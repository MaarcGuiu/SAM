Where do I find my tyre size?

It's printed on the sidewall of the tyre, in this format:

205/55 R16 91V
  1. 205 Tyre width in millimetres
  2. 55 Aspect ratio: sidewall height as % of width
  3. R Radial construction
  4. 16 Wheel diameter in inches
  5. 91 Load index
  6. V Speed rating

You can also find it on the sticker in the driver's door frame or in the vehicle handbook. If you can't find it, just tell us your car model and we'll check.
